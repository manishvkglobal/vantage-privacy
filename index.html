Privacy Policy — Vantage

Published by VK Global Group for the Vantage field operations app.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains how Vantage accesses, collects, uses, shares, and securely handles personal and sensitive user data (including account details, location, and photos). It applies to the Android app named Vantage (package com.vantage.vksnop) and related company systems.

Vantage is a workplace tool for authorized employees of VK Global Group (sales executives, area heads, and administrators). It is not a consumer social app and is not directed at children.

1. Who we are

The data controller for this app is VK Global Group. Questions, access requests, and deletion requests: manish@vkglobaldigital.com.

2. Personal and sensitive data we collect

We collect only what is needed to run field operations. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • Account and identity: name, work email or user ID, role, and password (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text).
  • Precise and approximate location: GPS coordinates when you punch in, start the day, or mark a school visit. Location is collected only while the app is in use for that action — not continuously in the background.
  • Photos and camera: school entrance photos and expense or receipt images that you capture or choose from the gallery as work proof.
  • Work records: schools, tours, visits, expenses, inventory and related field activity you enter.
  • Device: a login session token stored on the phone so you stay signed in until you sign out.

Some Android devices may also prompt for microphone permission because the camera library can request it. Vantage does not record audio and does not use the microphone for any feature.

3. How we use this data

We use personal and sensitive data solely to:

  • Authenticate you and keep your session signed in
  • Record attendance / punch-in and verify you were on site
  • Store visit proof (photo and location) for managers
  • Process expenses, tours, and other assigned field work
  • Let authorized administrators manage users, schools, and reports

We do not use this data for advertising, marketing profiles, or sale to data brokers.

4. How we share data

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with advertisers. Access is limited to:

  • Authorized VK Global Group administrators and managers on a need-to-know, role-based basis
  • Infrastructure operators who host our company servers in India, only as required to keep the service running
  • Google, solely to display map tiles in the app (Google Maps). Visit records themselves are stored on our company systems, not sold to Google for advertising

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the company, employees, or the public.

5. Secure handling of personal and sensitive user data

We apply the following procedures to protect personal and sensitive data collected by Vantage:

  • Access control: the app requires a company-issued account. Users only see data for their role and assigned work. Administrators provision and revoke accounts.
  • Authentication: passwords are stored using bcrypt one-way hashing. We do not store login passwords in plain text. Session tokens on the device are removed when you sign out.
  • Least privilege for sensitive data: location and photos are requested only when you perform the related work action (punch-in, school visit, expense proof), not on app launch for unrelated screens.
  • Purpose limitation: location is used as visit / attendance proof; photos are used as visual proof of the school or expense. They are not used to track you outside those actions.
  • No advertising SDKs: the app does not include ad networks, and we do not sell or rent personal data.
  • Storage: records are stored on company-operated servers used by VK Global Group (including hosting in India). Access to those systems is limited to authorized administrators.
  • Employee devices: you are responsible for keeping your phone and login details secure. Sign out if you leave a shared device.
  • Incident handling: if we become aware of a data incident affecting this app, we will investigate, contain it, and notify affected employees and required authorities as applicable.

6. Permissions the app uses

  • Location (precise / approximate, while in use): punch-in and school visit geo-tagging.
  • Camera and photos: school entrance and receipt images you choose to attach.
  • Internet: to send your work records to company servers.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep work records as long as needed for operations, audit, and legal purposes. When an employee leaves, their login is disabled. Employees may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by contacting their administrator or manish@vkglobaldigital.com. We may retain limited records where the law or an audit requires it.

8. Children’s privacy

Vantage is for authorized adult employees. It is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the applicable age), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. In-app disclosure

The same privacy policy is linked from the Vantage sign-in screen. Location and camera prompts appear in the app at the time those features are used, with a short explanation of why the data is needed.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
